PlantPulse

Most houseplants die from care by vibes - watered whenever someone remembers, which is a death sentence for a fern and overkill for a cactus.

PlantPulse gives every plant its own clock. Pick the species, log when you water or fertilize, and the app sorts your collection thirstiest-first so the plant that actually needs you comes first. Overdue tasks surface in red before the damage does.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
